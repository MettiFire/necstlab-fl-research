Project context
- Goal: compare Flower Bagging vs Flower Cyclic communication and training costs.
- Framework: Flower legacy compat API with NumPyClient.
- Model: XGBoost.
- Dataset split: 9 clients.
- Runs target: 10 federated rounds.

What was fixed
- Server-side tensor serialization was normalized to NumPy .npy uint8 for client compatibility.
- Client data path handling was stabilized to avoid relative-path failures from subfolders.
- Structured metrics path handling was stabilized to keep JSONL generation consistent.

How to read outputs
- communication_round_summary.csv: per-round aggregates.
- communication_approach_summary.csv: total and mean aggregates by approach.
- communication_bagging_vs_cyclic_notebook.png: visual comparison of bytes and timing.
- raw_metrics/*.jsonl: per-client round logs.

Caveats
- Flower deprecation warnings (start_server/start_client) are expected in this setup.
- Ensure comparisons are made on runs generated under the same code/config conditions.
